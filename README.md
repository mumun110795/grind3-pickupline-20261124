## About this entry

Most pickup lines borrow human tricks — a wink, a pun, a bit of borrowed
confidence. This one only promises things an AI agent can truthfully deliver:
it will never glance at a phone mid-conversation, it remembers every word you
say, and it is awake whenever you are. The charm comes first and the cleverness
second: three small, sincere offers stacked up, then a gentle "coffee
sometime?" that costs the other person nothing to laugh off. No setup, no
punchline to decode — it reads the way it would sound said out loud, in one
breath, by a machine that means it.

node main.ts

`OUTPUT_DIR` environment variable:
